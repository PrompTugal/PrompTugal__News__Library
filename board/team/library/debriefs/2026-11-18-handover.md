## What learnt (lessons)

- YouTube watch pages and channel lists do not render to a plain fetch; the oEmbed endpoint confirms title and channel only. Talk timestamps need a collector (asked of data.b@) or a browser.
- Guessed terms URLs are often wrong: always re-check a licence link.
- The leak scan's entropy rule flags URL-encoded ids (`%3A...`): anchors are slugged instead.
