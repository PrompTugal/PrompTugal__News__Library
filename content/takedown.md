# If you think something should not be here, we take it down while we talk

The library catalogues public material: it records titles, links, dates and what each item is about, and links to the source. It never copies the media or the text of others; every item keeps its source's licence, linked on its card.

If you made, published or appear in something catalogued here and think it should not be listed, or that its card is wrong:

1. Open an issue on the library's repository: [github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Library/issues](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Library/issues), naming the call number (such as WS26 2026/0001). Or, if you would rather not write in public, use [promptugal.news/report/](https://promptugal.news/report/).
2. We take the card down first, then look, answer, change what needs changing, and record what happened (without your details) in the corrections log of [PrompTugal.news's principles](https://promptugal.news/principles/).
3. A withdrawn item keeps its call number, marked withdrawn, so no other item ever takes its address.

This is not an official site of any event or organisation it lists.
