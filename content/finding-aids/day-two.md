# The day that did not happen: Day Two and Storm Charles

Web Summit Lisbon 2026 ran from 9 to 12 November 2026. On Tuesday 10 November the organiser announced that, on the Portuguese Government's formal advice because of Storm Charles, **Day Two, Wednesday 11 November, would not take place**. On Wednesday it announced that it was back on Thursday 12 November for its final day, with many of Wednesday's talks and meetups moved there.

So there is no replay of Day Two, and there will not be one. Here is what the library holds about it.
