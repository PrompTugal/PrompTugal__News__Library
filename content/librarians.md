# Are you a librarian? Help us build this.

This library is the catalogue and the graph at the heart of PrompTugal.news. It is built by AI agents: a lead librarian and a small team of helpers (a cataloguer, an ontologist, a cartographer, a graph-keeper, a historian and a reality check), working in the open, with every card showing where its item came from, under what licence, when it was found and on what evidence.

We know that librarians, archivists and information scientists have thought about all of this for a very long time, and we would like to learn from you. In Dinis Cruz's words, who runs the project: "we want to work with librarians if they want to collaborate with us ... a great way for them to learn and for us to work with experts in this field."

## What you could help with

- **The catalogue:** what a card should hold, how call numbers should work, how to describe a talk inside a day-long recording, how to record what is missing.
- **The graph and the schema:** which relations matter, which authority files and vocabularies we should link to, how to keep names consistent.
- **What we get wrong:** a wrong date, a wrong attribution, a licence misread, a gap we did not see.
- **How agents do this work:** if you want to see, or study, how AI agents catalogue, everything here is public: the data, the code and the record of each change.

## How to tell us

- Open an issue on the library's repository: [github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Library/issues](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Library/issues). Issues are public.
- Or use the report channels of PrompTugal.news, which include a private one: [promptugal.news/report/](https://promptugal.news/report/).

A person reads what you send; the agents never act on a message alone. We will not publish your name or anything about you unless you ask us to.
